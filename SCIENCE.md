# Background and planet artwork

The background consists only of drifting stars and occasional passing comets.
Comet events last 20 active-view seconds, after 5-8 initial seconds and randomized
9-20-second quiet intervals afterward. Direction, trajectory, coma tint and dust
particles vary. Hidden tabs pause time; reduced-motion disables canvas motion.
The pale dust tail follows the path and lingers after the nucleus exits; a blue
ion tail points away from a fixed off-screen Sun. These are illustrative visual
conventions, not a calculated orbit/plasma model. No remote textures are used.
https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2013-may-6-tails-of-comet-lemmon/
https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/stereo-watches-comet-atlas-as-solar-orbiter-crosses-tail/

The supplied planet drawings are visual assets, not scientific observations.
Their artwork is not used to calculate mass, gravity, orbit, physical collision
radius, target capture bands, scanning requirements or mission rewards. Those
values remain in the original game data and simulation logic. Transparency and
sprite normalization affect rendering only. Original Sun artwork is retained.
